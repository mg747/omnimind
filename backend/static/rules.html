Game Rules & Scoring

Solo Mode

  • Select a category and difficulty to generate an AI challenge.
  • Correct answers increase your Adaptive Difficulty level automatically.
  • Incorrect answers drop your difficulty down by one level.

Multiplayer Mode

  • One player hosts a Lobby and shares the 4-digit Entry Code.
  • The Host starts the match, broadcasting the challenge to all users simultaneously.
  • First to answer correctly gets +100 Points!

Expert Money Rewards 💰

  • Log into your Profile before playing.
  • Select "Expert" difficulty on a Solo challenge.
  • If you answer correctly on the first try, you earn $10 added to your virtual Wallet!
